**Cognitive Organics HR System** is a comprehensive, full-stack Human Resource Management platform built to modernize and centralize core HR operations. Designed with both administrators and employees in mind, the system streamlines the entire employee lifecycle — from onboarding through ongoing learning and document management — within a single, unified platform.

**Role-Based Access**
The platform supports three distinct user roles, each with a dedicated dashboard tailored to their responsibilities. Administrators have full system control, HR Managers handle day-to-day people operations, and Employees access their personal onboarding tasks, documents, and learning materials — ensuring everyone sees exactly what they need, nothing more.

**Employee Onboarding**
Administrators can create dynamic, customizable onboarding forms that adapt to different roles, departments, or employment types. New hires are guided through a structured onboarding journey, reducing confusion and ensuring no critical step is missed. The system replaces manual paperwork with a clean, digital-first experience from day one.

**Document Management & E-Signatures**
The platform integrates with Dropbox to handle document storage, sharing, and e-signature collection. HR teams can send documents for signing directly through the system, track signature status in real time, and maintain a centralized, organized document repository — eliminating scattered email threads and lost paperwork.

**Employee Learning**
A built-in learning management module allows HR teams to upload and organize training materials, company policies, and onboarding resources. Employees can access assigned learning content directly from their dashboard, supporting structured internal training programs and faster time-to-productivity for new hires.

**Centralized HR Operations**
By bringing onboarding, document management, and learning into one platform, Cognitive Organics HR eliminates the need for multiple disconnected tools. HR managers gain full visibility into employee progress, pending signatures, and training completion — all from a single control center.

**Key Highlights**
- Dynamic form builder for custom onboarding flows
- Dropbox integration for document storage and e-signatures
- Built-in learning materials and training management
- Three role-based dashboards for tailored experiences
- Centralized HR operations with real-time visibility
- Scalable architecture supporting growing teams
